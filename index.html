FMCG Budget vs Actual Analysis

Financial Planning & Analysis Dashboard

EFOS Global Finance Hackathon 2026 | Track 01 | Case 6

Project Overview

This project presents an Excel-based FP&A dashboard designed to evaluate budgeted versus actual financial performance of an FMCG business for FY 2025–26.

The analysis focuses on revenue, expenses, profit, profit margin, monthly budget variances and department-level transaction activity.

Key Findings

  • Revenue finished ₹3.31 lakh above budget.
  • Expense finished ₹0.61 lakh below budget.
  • Profit finished ₹3.92 lakh above budget.
  • Profit beat budget in 8 of 12 months.
  • July and August were the weakest months.
  • December and March were among the strongest months.
  • Expense control remained disciplined throughout the year.

Management Recommendations

  • Re-base July–August budget assumptions using actual seasonal performance.
  • Investigate September–December uplift for repeatable seasonal effects.
  • Prioritise deeper monthly variance review within Sales.
  • Maintain current expense discipline as a benchmark.
