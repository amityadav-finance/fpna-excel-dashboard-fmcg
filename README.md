# FMCG Budget vs Actual Analysis Dashboard

## EFOS Global Finance Hackathon 2026

**Track 01: Financial Planning & Analysis**  
**Case 6: Budget vs Actual Analysis**

### Project Overview

This project presents an Excel-based FP&A dashboard designed to evaluate the budgeted versus actual financial performance of an FMCG business for FY 2025-26.

The solution analyzes monthly revenue, expenses, profit and profit margin, identifies significant budget variances, evaluates department-level transaction activity, and provides management recommendations for improving future budgeting and financial control.

## Objective

To develop a financial dashboard that:

- Compares budgeted and actual performance
- Identifies significant monthly variances
- Analyzes revenue, expense and profit performance
- Highlights department-level transaction activity
- Explains key financial drivers
- Provides actionable management recommendations

## Dashboard Features

- Revenue, Expense and Profit KPIs
- Profit Margin analysis
- Monthly Budget vs Actual analysis
- Revenue and Expense trend analysis
- Profit variance analysis
- Department-wise transaction analysis
- Interactive Excel dashboard
- Monthly performance tracking

## Key Findings

- Annual revenue exceeded budget by 0.26%.
- Annual profit exceeded budget by 0.59%.
- Profit exceeded budget in 8 of 12 months.
- July and August were the weakest months due primarily to revenue shortfalls.
- December and March recorded the strongest profit outperformance.
- Expense performance remained disciplined throughout the year.
- Sales represented approximately 77% of total transaction activity.

## Recommendations

1. Re-baseline July-August budgets using actual seasonal performance.
2. Investigate the September-December revenue uplift to determine whether it represents a repeatable seasonal pattern.
3. Prioritize deeper variance analysis within the Sales function.
4. Continue monitoring Marketing and Operations as the major actionable expense areas.
5. Maintain current expense discipline as a benchmark for cost centres.

## Data & Methodology

The analysis uses 205 transaction-level records covering revenue and expense postings by month and department, together with a separate monthly budget dataset.

Key calculations include:

**Profit = Revenue − Expense**

**Variance = Actual − Budget**

**Variance % = Variance ÷ Budget × 100**

The dashboard consolidates monthly actuals and budget figures and presents the resulting financial performance through KPIs and charts.
